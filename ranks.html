Climb the academy

Every follower starts as Genin with a seat in the upper stands. A paid rank moves you closer to the field, unlocks gear in the shop and puts you in the daily fights. Paying never decides who wins: the comments do.

How joining works

  1. Pick a rank and join on Ko-fi.
  2. Write your Instagram @handle in the Ko-fi message.
  3. Your rank appears next to your name in the arena and your gear unlocks in the shop.

Ranks are monthly and you can cancel anytime. Gear you bought one-off stays yours; your rank goes back to Genin. The first 100 paid members are carved on the Founders stone at the arena gate. Under 18? Ask a parent before you pay. This is an original fan-made world, not affiliated with any anime.
