Terms & Privacy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

The short version. Ranks and gear are digital perks inside the Arashi Academy arena and its fight videos. Memberships are monthly through Ko-fi and you can cancel any time. We only keep your Instagram @handle and what Ko-fi sends us about your payment, and we never sell it.

1. What Arashi Academy is

Arashi Academy is an online ninja arena run on Instagram and on this website. Followers get a free seat in the 3D stands. Supporters can buy ranks and gear that change how their ninja looks and where they sit. Some members are picked for short fight videos posted on our Instagram. It is an original fan-style project and is not affiliated with Naruto, Masashi Kishimoto, Shueisha or any anime studio.

2. Ranks, gear and fights

  • Genin is free for every follower. Chunin ($3/month), Jonin ($5/month) and Sennin ($10/month) are memberships.
  • Single items (an outfit, weapon, jutsu or extra) cost $2 once and stay with your @handle.
  • Everything you buy is a digital, cosmetic perk: a seat, a look, items for your ninja and a chance to fight. Nothing has cash value or can be traded.
  • Fights are picked by us from active members. Paying raises your chances but does not guarantee a fight on any given day or a win.
  • Seats in the Master's lodge are limited. If a zone is full, you keep your current seat until one frees up.

3. Paying and linking your ninja

  • Payments go through Ko-fi and its payment partners (PayPal or card). We never see your card details.
  • To link a purchase to your ninja, use the code from the Unlock window (or your @handle) in the Ko-fi message, then send the same code to our Instagram from your own account. This proves the account is yours.
  • Your rank, seat and gear switch on once we confirm. If something doesn't show up within 48 hours, message us.
  • Keep your code private. Anyone with it can change your ninja's look.

4. Memberships and cancelling

  • Memberships renew every month on Ko-fi until you cancel. You can cancel any time in your Ko-fi account.
  • When you cancel, your rank stays until 35 days after your last payment, then you go back to Genin and your old Genin seat. Items bought once stay yours.

5. Refunds

  • Perks are digital and start as soon as we confirm your purchase. By buying, you ask for them to start right away.
  • Before we confirm your purchase, you can ask for a full refund for any reason.
  • After that, payments are not refundable, except where the law where you live gives you a right to one, or if we couldn't deliver what you paid for.
  • To ask for a refund, message us on Instagram or Ko-fi with your @handle and the date of the payment.

6. Behaviour

Fight dialogue is fiction. We may remove a seat, rank or handle that is offensive, impersonates someone or abuses the system, and refund any unused membership month when we do.

7. Privacy

  • What we keep: your Instagram @handle; your seat, rank, items and saved look; and what Ko-fi sends us about a payment (name you used on Ko-fi, email if you shared it, amount, tier, message).
  • Why: to seat you, unlock what you paid for, run the fights and answer you.
  • What's public: your @handle, seat, rank and look appear in the arena and may appear in our fight videos. Your email and payment details never do.
  • Who else handles it: Ko-fi and PayPal for payments, GitHub and Netlify to host the site and its data, Instagram for messages. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.
  • Your choices: message us to see what we keep about you, to change it, to be left out of fight videos, or to be removed from the arena completely.
  • The site keeps a few settings in your own browser (your last @handle and loadout). There are no ad trackers.

8. Changes and contact

If these terms change, we update the date at the top. Questions, refunds or data requests: message us on Instagram or through our Ko-fi page.
